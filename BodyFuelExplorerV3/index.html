Understanding the Explorer

See food as resources entering a living system—not simply numbers on a page.

01

What is this?

Your body constantly routes food, stored energy, and recovery resources against what you ask it to do.

The Body Fuel Explorer makes that internal process visible. Change the inputs, demand, or time—and watch the same system respond.

02

Start with a different question

Instead of beginning with “What diet should I follow?”, begin here:

What is the body trying to do with what I give it?

Brain activity, movement, repair, storage, and ongoing biological work all compete for available resources.

03

What could this help you explore?

  • How activity changes fuel use.
  • Enough energy, too little, or more than current demand.
  • Why protein, carbohydrates, and fat have different jobs.
  • Why repeated patterns matter more than one meal.
  • Body goals without turning nutrition into pass or fail.

You don't need to find the perfect setting. Explore the machine.
