FUEL INPUTS

Three inputs, overlapping jobs

Each input can support several destinations; the bright pathway shows its strongest emphasis in this model.

PROTEIN

Emphasizes rebuilding

Provides amino-acid building material while the body still needs energy to perform repair.

CARBOHYDRATES

Supports ready fuel + glycogen

Raises glucose availability and helps refill conceptual muscle and liver glycogen stores.

FATS

Supports use + longer reserve

Contributes concentrated energy and can also move into—or later return from—longer-term storage.

WHAT THIS MODEL IS — AND ISN’T

A relationship explorer, not a biological forecast

It shows

Generalized tendencies between fuel supply, tissue demand, storage, release and repeated patterns.

It does not show

An exact diet, measured metabolism, calories sent to one tissue, medical advice or an individual outcome.
